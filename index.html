Setup

  1. Download mbw9-v3.3.zip and extract all files into one folder.
  2. Launch Counter-Strike 2 in Fullscreen Windowed or Windowed mode.
  3. Right-click External.exe → Run as administrator.
  4. Press INSERT in-game to open the menu.
